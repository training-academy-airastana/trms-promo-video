# TRMS: look study from the supplied launch film

Reference: [Flowjam - Launch video, Bohdan Serdiuk, as labelled on Raivcoo](https://raivcoo.com/media/b3d0611b-949d-4760-9ab3-083d1744c860).

Inspected the 54.70-second, 1920 x 1080, 25 fps source at 0, 0.6, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3.5, 5, 7, 10, 13, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36, 40, 44, 48, 52 and 54 seconds. Download and extracted study frames are in the operating system temp folder. The source film is not used as a background asset in TRMS.

## Observed background

1. **Warm black substrate.** Dark areas sample around #110F0B and #12100C. The darkness leans brown/olive; blue is localized in the light. A navy fill changes the mood substantially.
2. **Adjoining optical facets.** Surfaces touch, with fine seams and gentle changes in brightness. They do not alternate between wide bright stripes and empty dark gaps. Most facets disappear into the unlit background.
3. **A continuous light field.** The lower-left light has an ivory/champagne core with blue on its falloff. At 2 seconds, representative pixels sample #D8CDBE in the warm light and #2A4E79 in its blue edge. These are compressed-video samples, not official brand colors.
4. **Refraction and spatial falloff.** The light is broken into slightly offset segments by the panes. The cream core fades through grey-blue to blue and then black. The light has no sharp circular boundary.
5. **Composition changes over time.** Around 0.6-2.5 seconds, the facets lean diagonally and light comes from the lower left. Around 3.5-5 seconds, the facets are vertical and the light broadens along the bottom. By 7 seconds there is also a field near the top. The illuminated region is an active part of scene transitions.
6. **Photographic finish.** Fine grain, soft bright edges and restrained glow combine the layers. Empty areas stay dark. Grain is texture, not visible floating dust particles.

## Typography and foreground

- Brief phrases have ample empty space, regular font weight and a small amount of glow. Blue, champagne and ivory appear across the lettering.
- Introductions resolve from soft focus with a small amount of travel; settled text is sharp. Some transitions bring the camera close before changing phrases.
- Product objects use silver surfaces, cool rims and warm reflected light. Their lighting agrees with the background.

## Adaptation for the approved seven-second TRMS opening

- Keep the existing cube assembly as the opening's central visual metaphor.
- Rebuild the backdrop with a continuous cream/blue light field sampled independently through adjoining panes. Use small refraction offsets, fine seams, a warm black base, and a grain overlay.
- Begin with diagonal light near the lower left; between 2.2 and 6.6 seconds, open the panes toward vertical and widen the light along the lower edge. This is an adaptation of the reference's behavior, not a frame-for-frame reconstruction.
- Keep the headline region dark. Move the small closing line below the headline so it does not sit in the bright lower light.
- Change the headline to lighter-weight ivory lettering, a blue/champagne emphasis and a short focus reveal.
- Give cubes satin silver/champagne and steel-blue materials, procedural studio reflections, a warm key and a cool rim.
- Follow-up: increase the assembly to 64 small cubes (4 x 4 x 4), and drive all text colors from the same light origin and unfolding phase as the backdrop.
- All motion is driven by the Remotion frame number. The grain uses a fixed seed. No wall-clock animation or random values are evaluated during rendering.

## Scope

This pass changes the existing seven-second visual opening. Audio, the remaining 33 seconds and final MP4 export are separate steps.
